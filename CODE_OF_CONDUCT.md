# Code of Conduct

Claudia Local follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Its full text is our code of conduct, for everyone in this project's issues, pull requests, discussions and other spaces.

In short, we pledge to make taking part a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Expected: being kind, respecting different views, giving and taking feedback gracefully, owning mistakes, and focusing on what's best for the people who use the app.

Not acceptable: sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insults, personal or political attacks, harassment in public or private, publishing someone's private information (including wallet addresses tied to a person) without permission, and anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems to the maintainers privately: open a [private report](https://github.com/claudia-onchain/Claudia-Local/security/advisories/new) with "Conduct" in the title. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and warn, suspend or ban anyone, following the Covenant's enforcement guidelines (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban).

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
